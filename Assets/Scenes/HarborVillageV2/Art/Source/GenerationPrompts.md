Create an entirely NEW production pixel-art architecture sprite atlas for a gothic medieval side-scrolling action RPG. Wide landscape 1536x1024 transparent RGBA, NO background. Middle third: broad three-storey merchant tavern with asymmetrical slate roof, wood-framed plaster, built-in market awning, barrels attached along base. Right third: squat harbor watchtower attached to a timber boathouse, slate roof and chimney, warm lantern. All ground-level doors 90-110 source pixels tall, buildings 480-760 source pixels tall, baseline y=930. The buildings must be at a HUMAN, believable scale, wide and compact instead of extremely tall narrow. Strict flat side-on orthographic elevation. Crisp deliberate chunky pixel art, limited cold desaturated blue-grey / muted purple / weathered dark oak palette, warm gold windows. High contrast readable silhouettes, coherent brick and timber scale. No ground/platforms below buildings. Fully opaque masonry with no holes or transparency except outside silhouettes. No text, labels, grid, UI, giant glow, scenery, cast shadows, water, people. This is for a game with a 32-pixel-tall knight; prioritize readable pixel clusters and grounded solid shapes.
